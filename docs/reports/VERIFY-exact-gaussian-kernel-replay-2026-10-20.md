## Mathematical target and independent oracle

With the same treatment and covariates in both outcome arms, let `delta = x - x*`. The independently drawn mediator contrast is `D = M(x) - M(x*) ~ N(a delta, 2 sigma_M^2)`. The direct-effect, intercept, and shared additive-covariate terms cancel, giving `Y(x,M(x)) - Y(x,M(x*)) = b D + epsilon_Y1 - epsilon_Y0 ~ N(a b delta, 2(b^2 sigma_M^2 + sigma_Y^2))`. Thus the tie-adjusted score is `Phi(a b delta / sqrt(2(b^2 sigma_M^2 + sigma_Y^2)))` when the variance is positive. When both terms in that variance are zero, it is 1, 0, or 0.5 according to the sign of `a b delta`. This requires the specified independent mediator and outcome errors and additive, common-scale Gaussian working model; it is not a general covariate-interaction or correlated-error result.

The standalone [verification script](verify_exact_gaussian_replay.R) separately integrates `E_D[Phi(b D / (sqrt(2) sigma_Y))]` for eight fixed fixtures (positive/negative paths, two null branches, reversed/non-unit contrast, unequal mediator/outcome residual variances, and zero mediator variance). Maximum absolute closed-form-versus-integral error was `2.22e-16` (required `<= 1e-8`). A million-draw direct normal-difference simulation differed from the formula by `5.452e-06` (prespecified sanity tolerance `< .002`). An additive factor-covariate and direct-effect perturbation changed the fitted score by `< 1e-12`. Invalid/nonfinite data, a one-level treatment, and an unsupported extra column returned `NA`; a deliberately nonfinite bootstrap value incremented the explicit failure count and made both interval endpoints unavailable rather than being discarded. The frozen Hopper `code/verify_kernel.R` independently passed against its production `code/kernel.R` (SHA-256 `56156c2c...`), reporting integral maximum error `2.22e-16`, direct-simulation error `0.0001905`, and passing guards. The replay fit uses residual degrees-of-freedom scales, as do I2/I6/e bootstrap fits; MBCO's likelihood MLE scale was not changed or conflated with this one.
